# Nexio CRM — User guide (English)

## 1. Sign in and secure your account
- Sign in with email and password. After 8 failed attempts the account is locked for that device for 15 minutes.
- **Settings → Security**: turn on two-factor authentication (scan the QR code with an authenticator app, then enter the 6-digit code). Save the recovery codes — each works once.
- The same page lists your active sessions; revoke any you do not recognise.

## 2. Customers
- **Companies** and **Contacts**: add records, link a contact to several companies, attach notes and files.
- **Tags**: create colour tags and put them on contacts, companies, deals, orders, projects or tasks to group and filter.
- **Leads → Deals**: qualify a lead, convert it, then move the deal along the pipeline.

## 3. Quotes
1. **Quotes → New quote**. Add lines (description, quantity, price) and an optional discount and "valid until" date. The total is recalculated on the server when you save.
2. A quote moves **Draft → Sent → Accepted / Rejected**; overdue quotes expire automatically. Only drafts can be deleted.
3. When a quote is **Accepted**, use **Create order** (once) to produce a draft order with the same lines and discount.
4. Price lists let you sell the same product at different prices; pick one on the quote.

## 4. Projects
- Create a project (or generate one from an order), add milestones and tasks.
- **Team**: add colleagues with a role (lead, member, viewer).
- **Discussion**: leave comments; you can delete your own, managers can delete any.

## 5. AI agents
- **AI → Agents**: start from a preset (Sales, Support, Ops, Analytics) and adjust persona, script and limits.
- Limits you set are enforced: discount and order-amount ceilings, daily/monthly spend caps, and a budget per agent and per campaign. A call that would exceed a cap is refused.
- An agent always says it is an AI; this cannot be disabled.
- Orders proposed by an agent are drafts and wait in **Approvals** for a person to approve or reject.
- Calls are currently a sandbox simulation; no real phone calls are placed.

## 6. Finance and integrations
- Invoices, payments, expenses and reports are under **Finance**.
- **API keys** and **Webhooks** (Settings) connect other systems; webhook deliveries are signed and retried automatically.

## Need help?
Contact your company administrator or Nexio Lab support.
