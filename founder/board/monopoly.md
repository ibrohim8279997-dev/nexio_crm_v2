# Monopoly lens
Score: 3 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- Local fit: Uzbek, Russian and English UI, Click and Payme in so'm, CBU rates and Telegram are things a foreign CRM serves poorly. This is the only candidate for a small market to own first (Uzbek SMBs leaving Excel and Telegram chats).
- Production on a server in Uzbekistan may help with data-residency buyers, if that is a real buyer requirement (unverified).
- Breadth (CRM, finance, helpdesk, portals, plugins) raises switching cost once a customer has data inside.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No durable edge, and the market is not yet defined.** The product is a broad feature list, the brief names amoCRM and Bitrix24 as competitors, and localization is copyable in months. There is no proprietary technology 10x better, no network effect, no scale economy and no brand (the trademark is unregistered). The two segments (retail and services) are not one small market owned first.
2. **Distribution is unproven.** One to five customers, no reference, and an unknown acquisition channel and cost. The lens says a product without a path to buyers is not a business. The first 100 customers have no known route.
3. **Core promise and trust gaps.** Real-time voice and AI calls are simulations, and the AI agents are the main differentiator in the upper plans. Backups sit on the same server, and personal-data rules are not reviewed by a lawyer. One outage or legal problem could end a CRM that holds customers' client data.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What do you know about Uzbek small businesses that amoCRM and Bitrix24 do not? What is the secret?
- Which one segment will you win first, and how many companies are in it? (Market size: open question, /founder-competitors.)
- How did the current 1 to 5 customers find you, and what does a customer cost to win? (/founder-cfo, /founder-consumer)
- What do customers pay today for amoCRM or Bitrix24? (/founder-competitors, /founder-consumer)
- What stops a copycat or a competitor's Uzbek pricing in month six?
- Which single module could be ten times better than the alternatives?
- Who else sells to the same buyer in Uzbekistan beyond the two named? (/founder-competitors)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable (vote is PASS). It would move toward FUND IF with: one named niche with a measured count of buyers, paying customers who agree to be references, a repeatable channel with a known cost, and one capability shown to be clearly better than the incumbents.
