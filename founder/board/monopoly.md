# Monopoly lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- A plausible small market to own first: Uzbekistan SMBs on amoCRM-style tools, with a local UI (Uzbek/Russian/English), Click and Payme billing, CBU exchange rates and Telegram integration. These are local-fit details that a global vendor is unlikely to prioritise.
- Distribution is partly built in: self-serve signup with a trial, and a "Nexio vs amoCRM" positioning document showing the founder knows who the switching target is.
- Switching-cost ingredients exist: tenant data, automation, webhooks, plugins, client portals (portal accounts do not consume seats). Once a company runs its pipeline and invoices here, leaving is costly.
- Timing argument is available (local payments and language support, AI sales features), though not yet made explicit.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. No proven 10x on anything. A CRM with contacts, deals, tasks, helpdesk and plugins is an incremental improvement on amoCRM and similar tools. The one differentiated claim, AI voice calls, is a sandbox simulation. Competitors can copy a feature list in months.
2. Distribution is unproven. Customer count, CAC and acquisition channel are unknown. Self-serve signup is not a channel. Without a repeatable way to reach the first 100 buyers, the product has no business behind it.
3. Pricing and market size mismatch. Plans of 89 / 199 / 349 per month (currency unconfirmed) may be high for small local companies, and the "small market to dominate" may be too small or too price-sensitive to support a broad suite. Breadth (CRM, finance, helpdesk, inventory, delivery, AI) with legacy code spreads a small team thin against narrower local rivals.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What do you know about Uzbek SMBs that amoCRM and local CRM vendors do not? What is the secret?
- Which exact segment (industry, size) will you own first, and how many such companies exist? (Open question: not stated.)
- How many paying customers today, what churn, what CAC, and where did they come from? (Open: all unknown.)
- What currency are the plans priced in, and what do local competitors charge? (Open.)
- What is better by roughly 10x for a buyer, measured how? What stops a copycat in month six?
- Team size, runway and budget? (Open.)
- When will real-time voice (ASR/TTS/SIP) ship, and is it the core bet or a distraction?
- Why now: what changed in the local market that makes this winnable this year?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Name one narrow segment and show a majority share, or a credible path to one, within it.
- Show a repeatable channel to the first 100 customers with a measured CAC (partners, Telegram communities, resellers or similar), backed by real data.
- Prove one thing 10x better for that segment (for example local payments plus Telegram-native workflows, or working AI voice) with customer evidence rather than a feature list.
- Show paying customers and retention numbers; disclose the currency and price test results.
- Cut or pause breadth (inventory, delivery, helpdesk) that does not serve the chosen wedge.
