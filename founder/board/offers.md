# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- A real market signal: small and growing companies in Uzbekistan already pay for amoCRM-style tools, so the pain is proven and the budget exists. Local payments (Click, Payme), CBU rates, Uzbek/Russian UI and Telegram are things an imported tool does badly.
- A clear anchor: positioning against amoCRM gives buyers a reference price and a reason to compare.
- Stackable assets already built: client portals that do not consume seats, inventory, delivery, quotes, invoices, and an AI assistant. Plan tiers are legible and a trial lowers the entry effort.
- Low cost to deliver most of the stack (software), so bonuses and a guarantee are affordable in principle.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. No proof of outcome (likelihood). The brief names no customers, revenue, churn or case study. Buyers have no reason to believe Nexio gets them more sales than what they use now.
2. Offer reads as a feature list at a price, not a result. Plans differ by seats, AI minutes and GB. That is a commodity "cheaper or broader amoCRM" pitch with a high effort ask (migrate data, retrain the team). The AI voice headline is simulated, so selling it risks broken trust.
3. Unclear market and reach. The exact segment, country of sale and acquisition channel are unstated, and whether the buyers can pay 89 to 349 per month (currency unconfirmed) is an open question. Without a niche, it is hard to find buyers cheaply.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which exact industry has the most pain, and who is the buyer (owner, sales head)?
- How many paying customers today, what revenue, what churn?
- What currency are the plan prices in, and what share of target firms can pay them?
- What is the one result you promise (for example more deals closed, faster replies), and in how many days?
- What does migration from amoCRM cost the buyer in time, and do you do it for them?
- What guarantee can you afford, and what is the cost to deliver it?
- How do the first 100 customers arrive, and at what cost each?
- Could you double the price for a narrow niche and still be the obvious choice?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Name one niche and one measurable outcome, with a time frame.
- Show at least a few paying customers or pilot results as proof.
- Package a done-for-you migration from amoCRM as the core of the offer, with a guarantee (for example refund if not live and in use within a set period).
- Do not sell simulated AI voice as a working feature until it works.
- Confirm price currency and willingness to pay with real pre-sales, not trials alone.
