# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- A real, named pain: companies tracking clients in Excel and Telegram chats, with amoCRM and Bitrix24 as the step-up they already know exist.
- Local fit is a genuine differentiator: Uzbek, Russian and English UI, Click and Payme billing in so'm, CBU rates, Telegram integration. This reduces effort for the buyer.
- Tiering is clear (Starter, Business "most popular", Enterprise), and the self-serve custom plan lets a buyer build exactly what they need.
- Some paying customers exist (1 to 5), so the offer has been bought at least once.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. Likelihood is near zero on the value equation. No reference customer, no proof, no guarantee. Selling "AI sales agents/calls" when real-time voice is not built (sandbox only) risks a broken promise on the headline feature.
2. The price may be mismatched to the market. $89 for the smallest plan is untested against what shops and service firms pay today. Whether the buyer has money at this level is unknown. The offer is a feature list, not a stack tied to one dream outcome (for example "more closed deals"), and the custom per-unit pricing invites price comparison, which makes it a commodity.
3. Trust and risk the buyer cannot see: backups on the same server as production, an unregistered trademark, and no legal review of personal-data rules. A buyer holding client data will ask about these.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which one segment (retail or services) has the pain and the money, and what result does it want in what time?
- What do your paying customers say they gained, in numbers? Will any agree to be a reference?
- What guarantee can you afford to offer?
- What do buyers pay today for amoCRM or Bitrix24? (open: /founder-competitors)
- Would buyers pay $89, and where does it break? (open: /founder-consumer, then /founder-pricing)
- What does one customer cost to win, and what is churn? (open: /founder-cfo)
- Market size and growth in Uzbekistan: unknown. (open: /founder-competitors)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Pick one segment and write one outcome-led offer with a named guarantee that /founder-cfo confirms is affordable (use /founder-offer).
- Remove or clearly gate unbuilt voice features until they work.
- Get at least 3 customers to give a measurable result or a reference.
- Test price on a buyer panel before keeping $89 (/founder-consumer, /founder-pricing).
- Add off-server backups and a legal check of personal-data rules.
