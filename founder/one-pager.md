# Nexio CRM: one page

**Verdict: Not yet** (computed). Simulated buyers: 1 of 20 buy (5%); the bar is 25%.

**Problem.** Small Uzbek companies keep clients in Excel, Telegram chats or a per-seat CRM, and moving to a new tool means weeks of manual work with no one to help.

**Customer.** Owners and sales heads of companies with 3 to 15 staff in Uzbekistan, on Excel, Telegram, amoCRM or Bitrix24. First segment to test: small shop and service owners paying per seat for amoCRM (one simulated buyer; not yet a proven segment).

**Offer and price (proposed, under test).** Cloud CRM in Uzbek, Russian and English, with Telegram, paid in so'm via Click or Payme. "Founding 50": free done-for-you data migration for the first 50 companies, a 30-day refund, month-to-month. Lean $25 (3 users, not built yet), Starter $49 (5 users), Business $199 (15 users), Enterprise from $349. AI voice is not included because it is not ready.

All costs, the blended price and the 2-to-50-account ramp are estimates: no real cost, team or customer data exists.

**Evidence.**
- Panel: 0 of 20 at $89 with no migration help; 1 of 20 at the new offer. Price and migration objections fell; "nobody I know uses it" rose from 5 to 13 of 19 passers. Simulated buyers, not customers.
- Competitors: Kommo, Bitrix24, shamCRM and 1C:CRM. shamCRM is local, already lists Click and Payme, and is the fastest to copy. No competitor was found offering a fixed-price done-for-you migration (not searched exhaustively).
- Board: 3 of 3 FUND IF, average 4/10.
- Real test: none yet.

**What the money is for.** About $3,000 startup (estimated): free migrations for the first 50 accounts, launch content, registration and legal; plus about $750 a month fixed. Founder pay is not included.

**The ask / next step.** Run the paid pilot from 16 Nov to 11 Dec 2026: about 10 demos, 3 paying accounts, 2 written references. Launch on 14 Dec only if that passes.
