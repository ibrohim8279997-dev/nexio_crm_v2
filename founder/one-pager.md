# Nexio CRM: one page

**Verdict: Not yet.** Year 1 loses money in the base case and 0 of 20 simulated buyers buy. The plan is a paid pilot to get proof, not a funding ask.

**Problem.** Small shops and service businesses in Uzbekistan run clients from Excel and Telegram chats. The CRMs they know (Bitrix24, amoCRM) are called complicated or slow to support. Moving data and retraining staff is the main reason they don't switch.

**Customer.** Owners and sales managers of companies with 3 to 25 staff in Uzbekistan. 1 to 5 companies pay today; none has agreed to be a reference.

**Offer and price.** Nexio CRM in Uzbek or Russian with Click and Payme built in. Founding 50, until 31 Jan 2027: Lean $29 (up to 3 users) and Team $49 (up to 5), our team moves your clients in for free, price locked 12 months, 30-day refund, backups copied off-server. AI voice is not included.

| Number | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Price (blended, estimate) | $45 per paying account-month |
| Contribution per account-month | $40.40 (90%) |
| Profit margin at plan (40 accounts) | 47% |
| Break-even | 20 paying account-months a month |
| Year 1 operating profit | -$1,160 |
| Startup spend | $2,900 |
| Cash needed before it pays for itself | $6,270 |
| Buyer panel | 0 buy · 20 pass |

All costs are estimates; the model includes no salaries (at $2,000 a month of team pay, break-even is 69 accounts and year 1 loses $25,160).

**Evidence.**
- Panel (simulated): 0 of 20 buy, both with the old $89 pitch and the new $29/$49 offer. Price passes fell from 13 to 4 but trust passes rose from 5 to 11. Buyers said an owner they know and can call who uses it would flip a no.
- Competitors: Nexio is the dearest at 5 users ($89 vs about $49 to $75 for Bitrix24, Kommo, Zoho and Pipedrive). The one gap the research supports is a hands-on setup with fast Telegram support. shamCRM targets the same region at a lower price.
- Real test: none yet.

**What the money is for.** About $6,270: free migrations for the first 50 customers ($1,500), demos and content ($500), registration, trademark and a lawyer's review of personal-data rules ($700), off-server backup setup ($200), and the cash to cover about 7 months of operating losses.

**The ask / next step.** Run the Founding 50 pilot from 14 Dec 2026: 60 owners contacted, 15 demos, at least 5 paid first months with 3 reference agreements by 31 Dec. If fewer than 2 of the first 15 demos pay, stop and rework the offer and price before spending more.

*The panel is simulated buyers and the numbers are projections built on estimates; real customers and real quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
